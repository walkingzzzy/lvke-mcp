# Source Quality Domain

Use `SOURCE.CLAIM.SUPPORT`.

Check source identity, authority, publication/effective date, whole-source hash, locator, fragment hash, claim coverage, conflicts, circular citation, and whether a secondary source is being used where a primary source is required.

For each material claim, bind a server-resolved fragment or state the missing-evidence reason. Judge semantic support explicitly and preserve uncertainty. A valid URL/hash/locator does not itself prove authority, freshness, or support.
