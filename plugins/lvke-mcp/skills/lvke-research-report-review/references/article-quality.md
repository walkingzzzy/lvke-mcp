# Article Quality Domain

Use `ARTICLE.LANGUAGE.LOGIC`; deep reviews also cover `ARTICLE.VISUAL.LAYOUT`.

Check grammar, typos, terminology, template residue, contradictory facts, unsupported logical jumps, headings, numbering, figure/table references, captions, pagination, overflow, clipping, blank/duplicate pages, and legibility. Compare repeated passages only as similarity evidence; never claim legal plagiarism.

For deep review, inspect every rendered page or record exact unprocessed pages as incomplete. Separate editorial P3 issues from decision-changing contradictions or missing arguments.
