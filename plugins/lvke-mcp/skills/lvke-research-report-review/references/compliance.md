# Compliance Domain

Use `COMP.SUBSTANTIVE.REVIEW`. Review the frozen national/Hubei requirement snapshot for investment approval, land, environmental assessment, energy/carbon, safety, procurement, and tax scope.

Check publisher, document number, official URL, effective date, replacement relation, project applicability, required approval/material, and whether the report states conditions as facts. Cite exact clauses or state why evidence is missing.

Stop with `incomplete` when the applicable-rule set or official/effective source cannot be established. Use `not_determinable` or `professional_determination_required` where legal interpretation or regulator judgment is necessary. Never write “approved”, “lawful”, or “compliant in all respects”; the allowed pass means only `conforms_to_checked_requirements`.
