# Feasibility Domain

Use `FEASIBILITY.DECISION.LOGIC`.

Trace market evidence to demand, demand to scale, scale to site/technical scheme, scheme to investment and schedule, operations to revenue/cost drivers, financing to cash flow, impacts/approvals to implementation conditions, and risks to the final recommendation.

Check alternatives, constraints, assumptions, downside scenarios, implementation readiness, risk ownership, and whether the executive conclusion can support a decision independently. A complete chapter list is not a complete argument chain. Mark unsupported feasibility claims P1 when they could change the investment decision.
